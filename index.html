Pre-Storm / Mitigation Reference

General planning prompts — verify against BHFD and LA County Public Works SOPs before acting; this is not a substitute for department policy.

  • Cross-check forecast QPF against known slide-prone segments of Coldwater Canyon, Benedict Canyon, and Beverly Glen — these canyon roads have a documented history of closures in major storms.
  • Coordinate early with LA County Public Works / Flood Control on debris-basin status above the canyons and any pre-storm cleanout needs.
  • Confirm sandbag/k-rail staging locations and readiness ahead of forecast thresholds, not after rain begins.
  • Identify structures below steep slopes or within CGS-flagged polygons for a proactive welfare check or advisory.
  • Review evacuation routes out of canyon areas for redundancy if a primary route floods or slides.
  • Track storm-drain and catch-basin capacity at known chronic flooding points.
  • Re-verify all of the above against current BHFD Standards of Cover and any active LA County OES guidance for the storm.
